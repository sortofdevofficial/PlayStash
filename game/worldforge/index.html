📖 How to Play WorldForge

🕹️ Camera & Controls:
• WASD: Pan camera across the world.
• Right Mouse Button + Drag: Rotate and tilt camera angle.
• Mouse Wheel / Q & E: Adjust zoom and camera height.
• R Key: Rotate building ghost before placing.
• Right-Click / Esc: Cancel active building placement.
🪓 Gathering Resources:
• Left-click Trees to harvest Wood / Wheat (🌾).
• Left-click Boulders to quarry Stone (🪨).
• Villagers will also wander and automatically harvest nearby nodes!
🛖 Building Your Village:
• Campfire: The heart of your settlement. Attracts villagers to join.
• Hut: Expands village population capacity (+2 villagers each).
• Farm: Produces Food (🌽) so your villagers don't starve.
• Well: Provides fresh Water (💧).
• Storage: Increases max resource stockpiles (+150 cap).
• Watchtower: Villagers climb up to scout, boosting nearby work speed!
👤 Villagers & Management:
• Click any villager in default mode to inspect their Hunger and Happiness.
• Press the 🎥 camera icon to follow them as they work and explore!
