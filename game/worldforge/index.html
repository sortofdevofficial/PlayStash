WorldForge - free 3D village builder browser game

What is WorldForge?

WorldForge is a cosy low-poly village builder that runs entirely in your web browser. There is nothing to download and no account required - you land on an empty meadow, place a campfire, and villagers start wandering in to help you build.

How to play WorldForge

Pan the camera with WASD, rotate it with the right mouse button, and zoom with the mouse wheel. Open the build menu to place buildings, press R to rotate a building before you drop it, and press Escape to cancel.

Your villagers chop trees for wood and quarry boulders for stone, so keep them fed and watered with a farm and a well, and build a lumbermill to turn out timber steadily.

Buildings

  • Campfire - the heart of the settlement; it attracts new villagers to your world.
  • Hut - raises your population cap so more villagers can live and work.
  • Farm - grows wheat into food for your villagers.
  • Well - draws the water your villagers need to stay happy.
  • Storage - raises the maximum stockpile for every resource.
  • Market - trades food and water for wood and stone.
  • Watchtower - a stationed villager speeds up everyone working nearby.
  • Lumbermill - a working saw pit. Assign up to four villagers to it: the more staff the mill has, the faster it cuts, and the faster its woodlot regrows. Once you own a lumbermill, new trees grow inside its woodlot instead of scattered across the wilderness.

Saves and visiting

Sign in to sync your world to the cloud, keep several worlds side by side, and browse the worlds other players have published so you can walk through them.

How to Play WorldForge

Camera & Controls:
• WASD: Pan camera across the world.
• Right Mouse Button + Drag: Rotate and tilt camera angle.
• Mouse Wheel / Q & E: Adjust zoom and camera height.
• R Key: Rotate building ghost before placing.
• Right-Click / Esc: Cancel active building placement.
Gathering Resources:
• Your villagers walk out to Trees and chop them for Wood.
• Others quarry Boulders for Stone.
• A Lumbermill cuts timber from its own fenced woodlot - click it to assign workers.
• Keep a route open to a node, or nobody can reach it.
• Trade at the Market when the wild runs down.
Building Your World:
• Campfire: The heart of your settlement. Attracts NPCs to join.
• Hut: Expands world population capacity (+2 NPCs each).
• Farm: Produces Food so your NPCs don't starve.
• Well: Provides fresh Water.
• Storage: Increases max resource stockpiles (+150 cap).
• Lumbermill: Grows and chops timber. Click it after placing to assign up to 4 workers - the bigger the crew, the faster the wood. Once you own one, new trees grow in its woodlot instead of the wild.
• Watchtower: NPCs climb up to scout, boosting nearby work speed!
NPCs & Management:
• Press the button to open the NPC panel and see everyone's mood.
• Each NPC shows their hunger, happiness, and current thought about the world.
• Press the camera icon on any NPC to follow them as they work and explore!
